Wayne's New Role And Kai's Football Ambitions Captured Brilliantly In 'The Real Rooneys' On Disney+

There have been some major changes in the Rooney family in recent times, with both parents and children entering new chapters in their lives both inside and outside the world of football.

After spending much of the last decade or so at home with the kids, Coleen Rooney has decided now is time for her to take on some new ventures.

It has been a very different story for husband Wayne, whose managerial ambitions took a hit with his exit from Plymouth Argyle in 2024.

All of these developments and more can be seen in The Real Rooneys, A new 10-part series now streaming exclusively on Disney+.

'The Real Rooneys' now available on Disney+

The Rooney household is looking quite different in comparison to what it had been in recent years.

Wayne now has more time at home, while Coleen goes in pursuit of a number of new business ventures. It certainly makes for an interesting mix.

Elsewhere, eldest son Kai is following his own footballing ambitions. The teenager is chasing his dream of becoming a professional footballer in the Manchester United academy.

All of those topics and more are covered in the official trailer for The Real Rooneys below.

Both start and end of football careers captured on 'The Real Rooneys'

Wayne Rooney is someone who knows what it takes to make it at the highest level of football, having been a teenage prodigy at Everton before carving out a spectacular career with Manchester United and England.

He is keen to offer advice to Kai as he chases a similar dream, but he won't be using his own name to open doors for the youngster. In the show, Wayne admits that he wants his son to earn his place on merit and not on the family name:

Kai wants his own legacy, and I'll be there to support him. What I don't want is him to get a scholarship or get a contract because of me.

I want it to be because of him in his own right. It’s something he's wanted for a long time, so [I'll] be proud, really proud of him to get a scholarship.

He will have plenty of time at home to get his message across in The Real Rooneys after his managerial career is brought to a halt, even if he does still have plenty of commitments in the world of football punditry.

As Wayne himself says in the series:

We spoke about [Coleen] doing more work, not about me getting sacked!

The first eight episodes of The Real Rooneys are now streaming exclusively on Disney+, with the final two episodes available on October 1st.
