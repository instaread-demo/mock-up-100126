The effectiveness of any supplement depends on its formulation. CitrusBurn combines traditional plant extracts with modern metabolic research. Let’s take a closer look at the key ingredients in this weight loss supplement:

Seville orange peel contains p-synephrine, a compound often used in stimulant-free weight management formulas. It may support fat oxidation and metabolic rate without affecting heart rate. This ingredient is commonly chosen as a gentler alternative to caffeine.

Apple vinegar has a long history of use for metabolic support. It may help regulate blood sugar levels and support appetite control. Stable blood sugar can reduce energy crashes and cravings throughout the day.

Andalusian Red Pepper

Red pepper extracts contain capsaicinoids that support thermogenesis. Unlike high-dose stimulants, this effect tends to be mild and steady. This ingredient may help increase calorie burn while supporting digestion.

Himalayan Mountain Ginger

Ginger supports digestion and metabolic activity. It may also help reduce inflammation that interferes with healthy fat loss. Digestive comfort plays an important role in long-term supplement adherence.

Ceremonial Green Tea Extract

Green tea provides natural polyphenols rather than heavy caffeine. These compounds support fat oxidation and antioxidant protection. The ceremonial-grade focus emphasizes purity over stimulation.

Berberine

Berberine is well known for its role in glucose metabolism. It may help improve insulin sensitivity and reduce fat storage signals. This ingredient supports weight management at the cellular level.

Korean Red Ginseng

Ginseng supports energy balance and stress adaptation. Chronic stress often contributes to weight gain and stalled progress. This ingredient helps support metabolic resilience rather than quick stimulation.
