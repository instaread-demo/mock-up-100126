Cumulus Fresno’s 93.7 KISS Country Raises Over $100,000 for Valley Children’s Hospital in Successful One-Day “KISS Country For Kids Radiothon”

FRESNO, CA — September 16, 2026 — Cumulus Media’s 93.7 KISS Country/KSKS-FM announces that its 28th annual KISS Country For Kids Radiothon on Thursday, September 10th raised $103,608 from generous Central Valley listeners to help children and families served by Valley Children’s Hospital in Madera, CA. The Radiothon raised awareness and drove donations for the local hospital, which has served the Greater Fresno area since 1952. All funds raised will benefit Valley Children’s Hospital.

The successful Radiothon leveraged 93.7 KISS Country’s powerful on-air and online platform reaching Country music fans across the Fresno area. The daylong broadcast event featured popular on-air personalities Kris & Joe, Erica Pierce, Greg Lane, and Jody Jo, who broadcast live from 6:00am to 7:00pm on Thursday, September 10th from the rotunda at Valley Children’s Hospital.

Andy Winford, Program Director, 93.7 KISS Country/KSKS-FM, commented: “I’m so proud of our air talent and our great partners at Valley Children’s Hospital and grateful we can be a small part of helping kids here in the Central Valley.”

For more information or to stream 93.7 KISS Country, visit: https://www.ksks.com/.
