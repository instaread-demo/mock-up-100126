8 Billings Coffee Shops You’ll Want to Try on National Coffee Day

Traci TaylorPublished: September 29, 2026
  • Share on Facebook
  • Share on Twitter
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 5:15
Instaread

Today is National Coffee Day, and I don't know about you, but I'm the kind of person who never needs an excuse to celebrate coffee.

Hot, iced, black, with cream and sugar, first thing in the morning or way too late at night before bed, there's a very good chance you'll find me with a cup in my hand. One of my favorite things about living in Billings is knowing that wherever I am, a good coffee stop is not too far away.

To put this list together, I used Yelp customer feedback, but this is not a ranking. Coffee is personal. Your perfect cup might be a simple drip coffee, or it might be a blended drink with whipped cream on top. I love them all, and I’m cheering for every one of them.

Black Dog Coffee House Has Multiple Billings Locations

Black Dog Coffee House is one of those places that comes up again and again when people start talking about coffee in Billings. Yelp reviewers keep praising the drinks and baked goods, along with the friendly service and atmosphere.

You can visit the original location on 24th Street West, stop by Black Dog at Roots on Poly Drive, or head to the 10th Avenue North shop. Any of them is an easy choice when you want to sit down with a coffee and take a minute for yourself.

Big Sky Coffee Roasters Is a Fast Local Stop on Grand Avenue

Big Sky Coffee Roasters on Grand Avenue is a solid local stop when you need a great cup without making coffee a whole event.

Yelp reviewers praise the consistent drinks and quick service, and they also mention the friendly staff and prices. Sometimes you need a fancy coffee date. Sometimes you need caffeine immediately before the day gets away from you. Big Sky fits that second mood beautifully.

Maple Moose Has Coffee, Dirty Sodas, and Plenty of Options

If your coffee order changes depending on the day, Maple Moose Coffee and Pop Shop is worth a stop.

Yelp reviewers highlight the iced lattes and friendly service, and the cozy seating makes it easy to stay awhile. The menu goes well beyond coffee, with tea and dirty sodas, too. It is a great place to meet a friend or bring the kids, and it makes a coffee run feel a little more like a treat.

My personal favorite is a hot Maple Moose. My husband, who isn't a coffee drinker, loves the Brooklesby. Our son loves anything with matcha or cotton candy in it.

Poly’s Place Coffee Has a Retro Feel and Drive-Thru

Poly’s Place Coffee on Poly Drive has developed a loyal following. Yelp reviewers regularly mention the friendly service and quick drive-thru, and they like the roomy seating and gluten-free choices, too.

It has a retro feel that makes it more memorable than your average grab-and-go stop. If you want a place to meet up or get some work done without being rushed, Poly’s Place is a comfortable choice.

Rock Creek Coffee Roasters Serves Downtown Billings

Downtown coffee lovers should keep Rock Creek Coffee Roasters at the top of their list. Located at 124 North Broadway, the shop roasts small-batch beans and serves coffee right downtown.

It is the ideal spot to slow down and catch up with a friend, and you can take freshly roasted local beans home for your own setup. Reviewers frequently note the welcoming staff and the care that goes into every roast.

Classy N’ Sassy Coffee Is a Billings Drive-Thru Favorite

Classy N’ Sassy Coffee has locations throughout Billings, along with Lockwood and Laurel, which makes it a staple when you need a quick, quality drive-thru pick me up.

The company’s locations include the Heights, Broadwater, 6th Avenue North, and Grand Avenue. Locals consistently praise the friendly baristas and fast service, while the menu includes traditional espresso drinks along with creative seasonal lattes and smoothies.

Mazevo Coffee Makes Coffee and Breakfast Easy

Mazevo Coffee has multiple Billings locations, which is handy for those of us whose coffee habit doesn't take a day off.

Yelp customers have singled out the coffee and a menu that goes beyond basic espresso drinks. It works for a breakfast stop or a specialty drink, and it is just as dependable when you are already out running errands.

Mountain Mudd Espresso Is Part of Billings Coffee Culture

Mountain Mudd Espresso has been part of the Billings coffee scene for years, and its many local kiosks make it easy to find when a coffee craving hits.

There's something very Billings about pulling up to a coffee hut and driving away with exactly what you need. Coffee fans appreciate the convenient locations, friendly service, and broad menu of espresso drinks and blended favorites.

Where Is Your Favorite Coffee Shop in Billings, Montana?

National Coffee Day is a pretty perfect reason to try a new place or make a coffee run for somebody you love. Tell us where you are getting your coffee this week and what you are ordering when you get there.

13 Reasons Why (in Moderation) Coffee Is Actually Really Great for You

Gallery Credit: Traci Taylor

Laura Margarita via Unsplash

Coffee Might Add Years On Your Life

This is pretty big! Study after study has shown that people who are regular coffee drinkers are less likely to die prematurely. As a matter of fact, coffee drinkers have been show to live longer than those who don't drink coffee at all.

Jessica Lewis via Unsplash

Coffee Doesn't Actually Cause Heart Disease or Stroke

So many people say that caffeine increases blood pressure which we all know is bad for our heart but there are studies that say coffee doesn't really increase your risk of heart disease at all. As a matter of fact, more research is leaning toward showing that coffee actually lowers your chance of having a stroke.

Bruno Emmanuelle via Unsplash

Coffee Boosts Our Adrenaline

When you find that you have to do something that will require you to push your body physically, coffee can help because drinking it increases epinephrine levels in your blood. Epinephrine is...adrenaline.

Thomas Park via Unsplash

Coffee Might Lower Your Chance of Getting Parkinson's

Studies discovered that people who regularly drank coffee had a 60 percent lower risk of getting Parkinson's than people who don't drink coffee. By the way, this applies only to caffeinated coffee, not decaf.

John Schnobrich via Unsplash

Coffee Is Packed with Essential Nutrients

One single cup of coffee is loaded with vitamin B2, vitamin B5, manganese and potassium, and vitamin B3.

Mike Kenneally via Unsplash

Coffee Might Protect Your Liver

Did you give your liver a workout when you were younger? Good news if you're a coffee drinker because it might lower your risk of cirrhosis in people who drink at least four cups of coffee every day.

Nathan Dumlao via Unsplash

Coffee Contains a Massive Amount of Antioxidants

Think you're getting a bunch of antioxidants by eating fruits and vegetables? You are, but not as much as you get from coffee.

Danielle Macinnes via Unsplash

Coffee Might Reduce Your Risk of Type 2 Diabeties

There are several studies which have proven that people who are regular coffee drinkers have a much lower risk of getting type 2 diabetes.

Jakub Dziubak via Unsplash

Coffee Might Lower Your Chances of Getting Some Kinds of Cancer

Coffee drinkers are less likely to develop liver and colorectal cancers which are actually the third and fourth leading causes of cancer.

Mario Ibrahimi via Unsplash

Coffee Punches Back at Depression

People who drink coffee at least four cups of coffee a day have been shown to be 20 percent less likely to develop depression.

Laureen Missaire via Unsplash

Coffee Might Protect You Against Alzheimer's

Researchers have found that people who consume coffee on a regular basis are at a much lower risk of getting Alzheimer's. How much lower? 65 percent!

Emre via Unsplash

Coffee Gives Us Energy and Helps Make Us Smarter

Not only does coffee give us the jolt of energy we need to keep going, but when we drink it, the caffeine travels to our brain and helps makes us smart. This is true!

Chade Madden via Unsplash

Cofee Helps Us Burn Fat

Take a look at the ingredient list of just about every fat burning supplement and guess what you'll see? You guessed it - caffeine. Studies have proven that caffeine helps burn fat.
