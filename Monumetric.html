If you previously had a website with Monumetric and would like to rejoin our network, we are more than happy to partner with you again! You may still have access to our delivery script and ad tags; however, it is important that you formally reapply. We will need to submit your site to our ad providers for approval again and be sure the ads.txt file is updated with all current ad providers that will bid on your inventory.

We make the process as easy as possible. If you still have your Monumetric Console login you can use that and you will see the following page.


On the following page, you will be able to select the site you want to reactivate or submit a new site.

If you do not have access to your console, please use this link to apply again.
