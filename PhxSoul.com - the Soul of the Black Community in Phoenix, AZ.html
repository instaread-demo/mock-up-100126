Phoenix Chapter of Jack and Jill of America Seeking Beaus, Belles for March 2027 Beautillion

Posted On 23 Sep 2026
By : Tremaine Jasper
Comments: 4
Tag: Beautillion, boys, Jack and Jill of America, rites of passage
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 0:45
Instaread

The Phoenix Chapter of Jack and Jill of America, Inc. will proudly celebrate the achievements of African American young men through its 24th Anniversary Biennial Beautillion on March 27, 2027 (more details about the Beautillion TBA).

This inspiring event honors outstanding individuals, ages 16 to 19, from the Phoenix community who excel in academics, leadership, and service. The Beautillion is the Phoenix Chapter’s signature fundraiser, designed to provide scholarships while fostering personal growth and college readiness.

Young men who are interested in participating as Beaus can click here to apply:

https://form.jotform.com/lynnfmackey/2027-beau-candidate-application

Young women in Arizona who are interested in participating as Belles are also able to apply here:

https://form.jotform.com/lynnfmackey/2027-belle-candidate-application

Click here to learn more about the 2027 Beautillion:

https://www.jackandjillphoenix.org/beautillion
