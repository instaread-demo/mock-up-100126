PLANS to transform Liscard town centre’s public spaces are moving to the detailed design stage.

Specialist design consultancy Buro Happold Limited has been appointed after a competitive procurement process.

Wirral Council say the consultancy will prepare detailed designs for improvements under the Liscard Regeneration Programme.

The consultancy team will now move forward with the necessary surveys and investigations, as well as preparing tender documentation for future construction works.

The project aims to create a more accessible and welcoming environment for residents, visitors and businesses.

Funding for the £225,586 contract comes from Wirral Council’s Local Regeneration Fund.

The authority's economy, regeneration and housing committee approved the funding earlier this year.

Its chair, councillor Mark Skillicorn, said: "Liscard is a key town centre for Wirral and the appointment of an experienced design team is a significant milestone in moving our regeneration plans forward.

"The public realm improvements will help create a more attractive and accessible town centre that supports local businesses, encourages more visitors and provides a better experience for everyone who lives, works and spends time in Liscard."

The committee recently approved plans to sell Dominick House to the owners of the adjacent Cherry Tree shopping centre, in a move which is hoped will lead to its demolition.

Public realm improvements are part of wider plans to support investment, improve appearance and function, and create opportunities for sustainable economic growth.
